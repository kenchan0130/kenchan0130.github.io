#### 保存する情報

#### 外部に送る情報

本アプリが自動で外部に送る情報は、クラッシュとエラーの情報だけです。送信先は [Sentry](https://sentry.io/)（Functional Software, Inc.）です。

本アプリの不具合を見つけて直すため、本アプリが異常終了したときやエラーが起きたときに、次の情報を送ります。

- 本アプリのバージョン、OS の種類とバージョン、端末の機種
- 直前に開いていた画面の名前と、本アプリの状態

通信にともなって送信先に届く IP アドレスと、そこから推定される地域は、送信先で保存しない設定にしています。問題文・選択肢・選んだ解答も送りません。

本アプリの「ご意見・誤りを送る」や「この問題の誤りを報告する」を押すと、8. のお問い合わせフォームを開きます。フォームの「アプリの情報」欄には、どの問題についての報告か、本アプリと問題データのバージョン、OS の種類とバージョンがあらかじめ入ります。

#### 学習の記録のバックアップ

- **iPhone・iPad**：有料の機能を購入した利用者の学習の記録（解答の履歴、模試の結果、試験日など）を、利用者自身の iCloud（Apple）に自動で保存します。保存先は利用者の Apple アカウントの領域で、開発者は読むことができません。本アプリの「設定」の「iCloud に自動で保存」でオフにできます。iCloud に保存した記録を消すには、iPhone の「設定」で、利用者の名前から「iCloud」の「ストレージを管理」に進み、本アプリのデータを削除してください。
- **Android**：学習の記録は、端末の設定で有効になっている Google の自動バックアップの対象になります。保存先は利用者の Google アカウントの領域で、開発者は読むことができません。

#### 保存期間と削除

Sentry に送った情報は、最長90日保存されたのち削除されます。端末の中の学習の記録は、本アプリの「設定」の「学習の記録をリセット」か、本アプリの削除で消えます。

アカウントの登録を必要としません。次の情報を iPad の中に保存します。

| したいこと | 方法 |
| --- | --- |

### 9. App-specific details

#### 個人情報保護士 試験対策 問題集・模試

##### Information stored

The app does not require an account. Learning records (answer history, accuracy, questions marked for review, mock exam results, exam date, and settings) are stored only on the user's device and in the user's own iCloud or Google backup, and are not sent to the developer. The app does not use ads or analytics.

##### Information sent outside the device

The only information the app sends automatically is crash and error information. It is sent to [Sentry](https://sentry.io/) (Functional Software, Inc.).

To find and fix bugs, the app sends the following information when it crashes or an error occurs:

- The error and where it occurred in the program
- The app version, OS type and version, and device model
- The name of the screen opened just before, and the state of the app

The recipient is configured not to store the IP address that reaches it through communication or the location estimated from it. Question text, answer choices, and selected answers are not sent.

Tapping "ご意見・誤りを送る" (Send feedback or report errors) or "この問題の誤りを報告する" (Report an error in this question) opens the contact form in section 8. The "アプリの情報" (App information) field of the form is prefilled with the question being reported, the versions of the app and question data, and the OS type and version.

##### Backups of learning records

- **iPhone and iPad**: For users who have purchased the paid features, learning records (answer history, mock exam results, exam date, and so on) are automatically saved to the user's own iCloud (Apple). They are stored in the user's Apple Account, and the developer cannot read them. This can be turned off with "iCloud に自動で保存" (Save to iCloud automatically) in the app's "設定" (Settings). To delete records saved in iCloud, open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, go to "iCloud" and "Manage Storage", and delete the app's data.
- **Android**: Learning records are included in Google's automatic backup when it is enabled in the device settings. They are stored in the user's Google Account, and the developer cannot read them.

##### Retention and deletion

Information sent to Sentry is kept for up to 90 days and then deleted. Learning records on the device are deleted with "学習の記録をリセット" (Reset learning records) in the app's "設定" (Settings) or by deleting the app.

#### Mojimachi

Mojimachi helps children aged about 4 to 7 practice writing letters on an iPad. The child-facing screens and voices are in Japanese. This section is written for parents and guardians. The app is offered in the Kids category (ages 5 and under) of the App Store.

##### The short version

- There are no ads.
- There is no usage analytics and no tracking.
- The app does not collect a child's name, address, email address, location, photos, or voice.
- Learning records stay on the iPad and in the parent's own iCloud. The developer cannot read them.
- The developer receives only two things: crash information that does not identify anyone, and messages a parent chooses to send.

##### Information stored

The app does not require an account. It stores this on the iPad:

- A nickname (chosen by the parent; it does not have to be a real name), a face picture, writing hand, daily goal, and pen color
- Practice records (letters written, dates, judgment results)
- The shape of the lines the child drew (for each letter, the first time and a few recent times)
- Collected items, the town layout, and clothes worn
- A record of when the child tapped "ask a grown-up" on a locked letter
- Settings (judgment strictness, volume, captions, and so on)

If the iPad is signed in to iCloud and "Sync with iCloud" is on in the parent area, the app also stores these records in the parent's own iCloud private database (CloudKit, Apple).

- This is storage in the parent's own Apple Account. The developer cannot read it.
- It is for restoring records when the app is reinstalled or the iPad is replaced. iPads on the same Apple Account also sync with each other.
- Nothing is shared with other family members or other people.
- You can turn "Sync with iCloud" off at any time (parent area > Manage Records).
- If the iPad is not signed in to iCloud, records stay on the iPad only.

##### Information sent outside the device

###### Crash reports

Only when the app crashes, it sends crash information to [Sentry](https://sentry.io/) (Functional Software, Inc.). It is used to fix bugs. The data is stored in the United States (Sentry's US region).

What is sent:

- Where the crash happened (the place in the program)
- The app version
- The iPadOS version
- The general model family of the iPad

What is not sent:

- Nicknames, names, practice records, or drawn lines
- Any ID that identifies a person or an iPad
- Logs of taps, screen recordings, or performance measurements

The recipient is configured not to store the IP address that reaches it through communication. There is no setting to turn this off. Nothing is sent unless the app crashes. The crash information is not linked to a person or a child, and is not used for tracking or advertising. The parent area (under "Safety Settings") explains this too.

###### Contact form

From the parent area, a parent can open the contact form in section 8. It opens in Safari. A parent decides whether to send it.

- The text you write and an optional email address reach the developer.
- The form is prefilled with the app name, the type of message, the app version, and the iPadOS version. If you open it from "This judgment looks wrong", the name of the judgment setting is also filled in. The device model and any device identifier are not included. You can delete anything before you send.
- Please do not write a child's name or send a child's photo.
- We use what you send only to reply. We delete it within 90 days after we finish the matter. We also delete it on request.

###### Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment details such as card numbers, and they never reach the developer. The app only checks which products were purchased, using Apple's StoreKit.

##### Ads, analytics, and tracking

- The app shows no ads and includes no ad services.
- It includes no usage analytics service.
- It does not track people across other companies' apps or websites.
- The only numbers the developer sees are the summaries in App Store Connect, provided by Apple.

##### Children's information

Children aged 5 and under may use this app.

- The app does not let a child type a name, address, email address, phone number, location, photo, or voice. There is no screen for it.
- The child-facing screens show no external links, no purchases, and no contact form.
- External links, purchases, settings changes, and viewing learning records are available only after a parent passes the parental gate (a multiplication question).
- The nickname is chosen by the parent and stays on the iPad and in the parent's iCloud.
- The app is built so that the developer does not collect personal information from children, in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the principles of Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information.

##### iPad features the app does not use

The app does not use location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or calendar. If a parent turns on "Also ask for the iPad passcode" in the parental gate, the app uses the iPad's own authentication (Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode). Face and fingerprint data stay on the iPad and are never given to the app or the developer.

##### Retention and deletion

Information sent to Sentry is kept for up to 90 days and then deleted. Messages sent through the contact form are deleted within 90 days after we finish the matter.
